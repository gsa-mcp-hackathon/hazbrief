# Jack Rabbit II observed arc maxima: what the held 17-model manuscript tabulates (HB-192 ADDENDUM-05 item 5)

Read 2026-11-17 from the held PDF `mazzola-2020-jack-rabbit-ii-17-dense-gas-models-manuscript`
(`corpus/raw/`, its text not in the corpus index; `pdftotext -layout`, printed page = PDF page).

**Trial 7: tabulated.** Table 5, p. 20, "17 model predictions and observations of arc max C for Trial 7 for the six
downwind distances", has an `Observed` row, ppm at 0.2, 0.5, 1, 2, 5 and 11 km: 72580, 22686, 2621, 1417, 373, 50.
The table marks saturated observations in orange (p. 20); the colour is not in the extracted text, so which of the
six are saturated is read from the page image, not from this note.

**Trials 1 and 6: plotted only.** Figures 3 and 4 (pp. 21 and 22) plot the observed arc maxima for Trials 1 and 6
("observations (averaging times of 1 – 3 s) are indicated by the X's connected by a dashed line"; saturated at 0.2
and 5 km in Trial 1, at 0.5 and 5 km in Trial 6). No table in the manuscript gives them. Table 6 (p. 24) gives only
counts of over-, within- and under-prediction by a factor of two for each model over the three trials.

**What else the manuscript holds for an evaluation:** the recommended meteorological inputs for Trials 1, 6 and 7
(Table 2, p. 6) and the averaged simplified emission rates and parameters (Table 3, p. 7); the BOOT scores of every
model over 0.2 to 5 km (Table 7, p. 26), HPAC's and ALOHA's among them; MG's direction ("MG < 1.0 indicates an
overprediction mean bias and MG > 1.0 indicates an underprediction mean bias", p. 25).

**So:** an evaluation on Trial 7's arc maxima is possible from what is held. For Trials 1 and 6 the observed maxima
are to be acquired: Chang et al. (2021), which Fox et al. 2021 and Weil and Alessandrini 2022 cite for them
(`docs/evidence/hb195/jr2_reference.tsv`), through the acquisition ladder; or digitised from Figures 3 and 4, which
loses precision on a log axis.
