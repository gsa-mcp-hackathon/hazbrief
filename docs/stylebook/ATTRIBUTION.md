# Data sources and attribution

hazbrief-mcp reads public federal data. The notices below are the ones each source's terms ask for,
as catalogued in `corpus/api_catalog.yaml` (HB-06) and decided in DN-003 section 2. No agency named
here endorses, certifies or is affiliated with this server, and none of their logos or seals is
used.

## U.S. Census Bureau

This product uses the Census Bureau Data API but is not endorsed or certified by the Census Bureau.

Daytime worker counts come from LEHD Origin-Destination Employment Statistics (LODES). Requested
citation form:

U.S. Census Bureau, "{File Name}," LEHD Origin-Destination Employment Statistics, {Vintage}, https://lehd.ces.census.gov/data/lodes, accessed on {Date}.

Boundaries come from the Census TIGERweb services. Source: U.S. Census Bureau.

## U.S. Geological Survey

Facility locations come from The National Map Structures service. Credit: U.S. Geological Survey.
USGS-authored or produced data are in the U.S. public domain.

## National Weather Service

Wind at the incident point comes from the National Weather Service API (api.weather.gov). NWS web
content is in the public domain unless specifically noted otherwise, and is provided as is. No
endorsement by or affiliation with NOAA or the NWS is implied.

## Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services

Licensed bed counts come from the CMS Provider of Services data (data.cms.gov). Works of the U.S.
Government are in the public domain; no government endorsement is implied (42 U.S.C. 1320b-10).

## Emergency Response Guidebook

Initial isolation distances, protective action distances and guide text come from the Emergency
Response Guidebook 2024 (ERG2024), U.S. Department of Transportation, Pipeline and Hazardous
Materials Safety Administration. The server reproduces table values and guide text without
modification.
