ERG 2024 Guide 144: Oxidizers (Water-Reactive)

Potential hazards

FIRE OR EXPLOSION

  • May ignite combustibles (wood, paper, oil, clothing, etc.). [erg2024-en-pdf#p220]
  • React vigorously and/or explosively with water. [erg2024-en-pdf#p220]
  • Produce toxic and/or corrosive substances on contact with water. [erg2024-en-pdf#p220]
  • Flammable/toxic gases may accumulate in tanks and hopper cars. [erg2024-en-pdf#p220]
  • Some may produce flammable hydrogen gas upon contact with metals. [erg2024-en-pdf#p220]
  • Containers may explode when heated. [erg2024-en-pdf#p220]
  • Runoff may create fire or explosion hazard. [erg2024-en-pdf#p220]

HEALTH

  • TOXIC; inhalation or contact with vapor, substance, or decomposition products may cause severe injury or death. [erg2024-en-pdf#p220]
  • Fire will produce irritating, corrosive and/or toxic gases. [erg2024-en-pdf#p220]
  • Runoff from fire control or dilution water may cause environmental contamination. [erg2024-en-pdf#p220]

Public safety

  • CALL 911. Then call emergency response telephone number on shipping paper. If shipping paper not available or no answer, refer to appropriate telephone number listed on the inside back cover. [erg2024-en-pdf#p220]
  • Keep unauthorized personnel away. [erg2024-en-pdf#p220]
  • Stay upwind, uphill and/or upstream. [erg2024-en-pdf#p220]
  • Ventilate closed spaces before entering, but only if properly trained and equipped. [erg2024-en-pdf#p220]

PROTECTIVE CLOTHING

  • Wear positive pressure self-contained breathing apparatus (SCBA). [erg2024-en-pdf#p220]
  • Wear chemical protective clothing that is specifically recommended by the manufacturer when there is NO RISK OF FIRE. [erg2024-en-pdf#p220]
  • Structural firefighters' protective clothing provides thermal protection but only limited chemical protection. [erg2024-en-pdf#p220]

Immediate precautionary measure

  • Isolate spill or leak area in all directions for at least 50 meters (150 feet) for liquids and at least 25 meters (75 feet) for solids. [erg2024-en-pdf#p220]

  • For non-highlighted materials: increase the immediate precautionary measure distance, in the downwind direction, as necessary. [erg2024-en-pdf#p220]

Emergency response

FIRE

  • DO NOT USE WATER OR FOAM. [erg2024-en-pdf#p221]

Small Fire

  • Dry chemical, soda ash or lime. [erg2024-en-pdf#p221]

Large Fire

  • DRY sand, dry chemical, soda ash or lime or withdraw from area and let fire burn. [erg2024-en-pdf#p221]
  • Do not move cargo or vehicle if cargo has been exposed to heat. [erg2024-en-pdf#p221]
  • If it can be done safely, move undamaged containers away from the area around the fire. [erg2024-en-pdf#p221]

Fire Involving Tanks, Rail Tank Cars or Highway Tanks

  • Fight fire from maximum distance or use unmanned master stream devices or monitor nozzles. [erg2024-en-pdf#p221]
  • Cool containers with flooding quantities of water until well after fire is out. [erg2024-en-pdf#p221]
  • Withdraw immediately in case of rising sound from venting safety devices or discoloration of tank. [erg2024-en-pdf#p221]
  • ALWAYS stay away from tanks in direct contact with flames. [erg2024-en-pdf#p221]

SPILL OR LEAK

  • Do not touch damaged containers or spilled material unless wearing appropriate protective clothing. [erg2024-en-pdf#p221]
