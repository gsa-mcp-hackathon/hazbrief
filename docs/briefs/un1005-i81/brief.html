ERG 2024 Guide 125: Gases - Toxic and/or Corrosive

Potential hazards

HEALTH

  • TOXIC and/or CORROSIVE; may be fatal if inhaled, ingested or absorbed through skin. [erg2024-en-pdf#p182]
  • Vapors are extremely irritating and corrosive. [erg2024-en-pdf#p182]
  • Contact with gas or liquefied gas may cause burns, severe injury and/or frostbite. [erg2024-en-pdf#p182]
  • Fire will produce irritating, corrosive and/or toxic gases. [erg2024-en-pdf#p182]
  • Runoff from fire control or dilution water may cause environmental contamination. [erg2024-en-pdf#p182]

FIRE OR EXPLOSION

  • Some may burn but none ignite readily. [erg2024-en-pdf#p182]
  • Vapors from liquefied gas are initially heavier than air and spread along ground. [erg2024-en-pdf#p182]
  • Some of these materials may react violently with water. [erg2024-en-pdf#p182]
  • Cylinders exposed to fire may vent and release toxic and/or corrosive gas through pressure relief devices. [erg2024-en-pdf#p182]
  • Containers may explode when heated. [erg2024-en-pdf#p182]
  • Ruptured cylinders may rocket. [erg2024-en-pdf#p182]
  • For UN1005: Anhydrous ammonia, at high concentrations in confined spaces, presents a flammability risk if a source of ignition is introduced. [erg2024-en-pdf#p182]

Public safety

  • CALL 911. Then call emergency response telephone number on shipping paper. If shipping paper not available or no answer, refer to appropriate telephone number listed on the inside back cover. [erg2024-en-pdf#p182]
  • Keep unauthorized personnel away. [erg2024-en-pdf#p182]
  • Stay upwind, uphill and/or upstream. [erg2024-en-pdf#p182]
  • Many gases are heavier than air and will spread along the ground and collect in low or confined areas (sewers, basements, tanks, etc.). [erg2024-en-pdf#p182]
  • Ventilate closed spaces before entering, but only if properly trained and equipped. [erg2024-en-pdf#p182]

PROTECTIVE CLOTHING

  • Wear positive pressure self-contained breathing apparatus (SCBA). [erg2024-en-pdf#p182]
  • Wear chemical protective clothing that is specifically recommended by the manufacturer when there is NO RISK OF FIRE. [erg2024-en-pdf#p182]
  • Structural firefighters' protective clothing provides thermal protection but only limited chemical protection. [erg2024-en-pdf#p182]

EVACUATION

Immediate precautionary measure

  • Isolate spill or leak area for at least 100 meters (330 feet) in all directions. [erg2024-en-pdf#p182]

Spill

  • For highlighted materials: see Table 1 - Initial Isolation and Protective Action Distances. [erg2024-en-pdf#p182]
  • For non-highlighted materials: increase the immediate precautionary measure distance, in the downwind direction, as necessary. [erg2024-en-pdf#p182]

Emergency response

FIRE

Small Fire

  • Dry chemical or CO2. [erg2024-en-pdf#p183]

Large Fire

  • Water spray, fog or regular foam. [erg2024-en-pdf#p183]
  • If it can be done safely, move undamaged containers away from the area around the fire. [erg2024-en-pdf#p183]
  • Do not get water inside containers. [erg2024-en-pdf#p183]
  • Damaged cylinders should be handled only by specialists. [erg2024-en-pdf#p183]

Fire Involving Tanks

  • Fight fire from maximum distance or use unmanned master stream devices or monitor nozzles. [erg2024-en-pdf#p183]
  • Cool containers with flooding quantities of water until well after fire is out. [erg2024-en-pdf#p183]
  • Do not direct water at source of leak or safety devices; icing may occur. [erg2024-en-pdf#p183]
  • Withdraw immediately in case of rising sound from venting safety devices or discoloration of tank. [erg2024-en-pdf#p183]
  • ALWAYS stay away from tanks in direct contact with flames. [erg2024-en-pdf#p183]

SPILL OR LEAK

  • Do not touch or walk through spilled material. [erg2024-en-pdf#p183]
  • Stop leak if you can do it without risk. [erg2024-en-pdf#p183]
  • If possible, turn leaking containers so that gas escapes rather than liquid. [erg2024-en-pdf#p183]
  • Prevent entry into waterways, sewers, basements or confined areas. [erg2024-en-pdf#p183]
  • Do not direct water at spill or source of leak. [erg2024-en-pdf#p183]
  • Use water spray to reduce vapors or divert vapor cloud drift. Avoid allowing water runoff to contact spilled material. [erg2024-en-pdf#p183]
  • Isolate area until gas has dispersed. [erg2024-en-pdf#p183]

Refer to the “General First Aid” section.

  • In case of skin contact with hydrogen fluoride, anhydrous (UN1052), if calcium gluconate gel is available, rinse 5 minutes, then apply gel. Otherwise, continue rinsing until medical treatment is available. [erg2024-en-pdf#p183]
