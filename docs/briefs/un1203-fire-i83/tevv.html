T50 Which engine models the release: dense, passive or not classed, by the critical Richardson number

What it is
  • Which engine models the release: dense, passive or not classed, by the critical Richardson number.
Method and its source
  • Ri_c = H g (rho - rho_a) / (rho_a U*^2), with H the source's characteristic dimension (a continuous source: H = sqrt(E pi / (4 rho U10)); a puddle: H = E / (rho U10 D)) and U* from the surface-layer profile; below 1 the release is passive (NOAA Technical Memorandum NOS OR&R 43, ALOHA (Areal Locations of Hazardous Atmospheres) 5.4.4 Technical Documentation (November 2013), section 4.4.1, pp. 46 to 47, and section 4.2, pp. 40 to 41). A dense release is modelled by section T45's engine and never by the puff; a passive one by the puff; one not classed by neither (DN-026).
Inputs for this brief and where each came from
