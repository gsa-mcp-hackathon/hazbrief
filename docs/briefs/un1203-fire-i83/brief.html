ERG 2024 Guide 128: Flammable Liquids (Water-Immiscible)

Potential hazards

FIRE OR EXPLOSION

  • HIGHLY FLAMMABLE: Will be easily ignited by heat, sparks or flames. [erg2024-en-pdf#p188]
  • Vapors may form explosive mixtures with air. [erg2024-en-pdf#p188]
  • Vapors may travel to source of ignition and flash back. [erg2024-en-pdf#p188]
  • Most vapors are heavier than air. They will spread along the ground and collect in low or confined areas (sewers, basements, tanks, etc.). [erg2024-en-pdf#p188]
  • Vapor explosion hazard indoors, outdoors or in sewers. [erg2024-en-pdf#p188]
  • Runoff to sewer may create fire or explosion hazard. [erg2024-en-pdf#p188]
  • Containers may explode when heated. [erg2024-en-pdf#p188]
  • Many liquids will float on water. [erg2024-en-pdf#p188]
  • Substance may be transported hot. [erg2024-en-pdf#p188]
  • For hybrid vehicles, GUIDE 147 (lithium ion or sodium ion batteries) or GUIDE 138 (sodium batteries) should also be consulted. [erg2024-en-pdf#p188]
  • If molten aluminum is involved, refer to GUIDE 169. [erg2024-en-pdf#p188]

HEALTH

  • CAUTION: Petroleum crude oil (UN1267) may contain TOXIC hydrogen sulphide gas. [erg2024-en-pdf#p188]
  • Inhalation or contact with material may irritate or burn skin and eyes. [erg2024-en-pdf#p188]
  • Fire may produce irritating, corrosive and/or toxic gases. [erg2024-en-pdf#p188]
  • Vapors may cause dizziness or asphyxiation, especially when in closed or confined areas. [erg2024-en-pdf#p188]
  • Runoff from fire control or dilution water may cause environmental contamination. [erg2024-en-pdf#p188]

Public safety

  • CALL 911. Then call emergency response telephone number on shipping paper. If shipping paper not available or no answer, refer to appropriate telephone number listed on the inside back cover. [erg2024-en-pdf#p188]
  • Keep unauthorized personnel away. [erg2024-en-pdf#p188]
  • Stay upwind, uphill and/or upstream. [erg2024-en-pdf#p188]
  • Ventilate closed spaces before entering, but only if properly trained and equipped. [erg2024-en-pdf#p188]

PROTECTIVE CLOTHING

  • Wear positive pressure self-contained breathing apparatus (SCBA). [erg2024-en-pdf#p188]
  • Structural firefighters' protective clothing provides thermal protection but only limited chemical protection. [erg2024-en-pdf#p188]

EVACUATION

Immediate precautionary measure

  • Isolate spill or leak area for at least 50 meters (150 feet) in all directions. [erg2024-en-pdf#p188]

Large Spill

  • Consider initial downwind evacuation for at least 300 meters (1000 feet). [erg2024-en-pdf#p188]

Fire

  • If tank, rail tank car or highway tank is involved in a fire, ISOLATE for 800 meters (1/2 mile) in all directions; also, consider initial evacuation for 800 meters (1/2 mile) in all directions. [erg2024-en-pdf#p188]

Emergency response

FIRE

  • CAUTION: The majority of these products have a very low flash point. Use of water spray when fighting fire may be inefficient. [erg2024-en-pdf#p189]
  • CAUTION: For mixtures containing alcohol or polar solvent, alcohol-resistant foam may be more effective. [erg2024-en-pdf#p189]

Small Fire

  • Dry chemical, CO2, water spray or regular foam. If regular foam is ineffective or unavailable, use alcohol-resistant foam. [erg2024-en-pdf#p189]

Large Fire

  • Water spray, fog or regular foam. If regular foam is ineffective or unavailable, use alcohol-resistant foam. [erg2024-en-pdf#p189]
  • Avoid aiming straight or solid streams directly onto the product. [erg2024-en-pdf#p189]
  • If it can be done safely, move undamaged containers away from the area around the fire. [erg2024-en-pdf#p189]

Fire Involving Tanks, Rail Tank Cars or Highway Tanks

  • Fight fire from maximum distance or use unmanned master stream devices or monitor nozzles. [erg2024-en-pdf#p189]
  • Cool containers with flooding quantities of water until well after fire is out. [erg2024-en-pdf#p189]
  • For petroleum crude oil, do not spray water directly into a breached tank car. This can lead to a dangerous boil over. [erg2024-en-pdf#p189]
  • Withdraw immediately in case of rising sound from venting safety devices or discoloration of tank. [erg2024-en-pdf#p189]
  • ALWAYS stay away from tanks in direct contact with flames. [erg2024-en-pdf#p189]
  • For massive fire, use unmanned master stream devices or monitor nozzles; if this is impossible, withdraw from area and let fire burn. [erg2024-en-pdf#p189]

SPILL OR LEAK

  • ELIMINATE all ignition sources (no smoking, flares, sparks or flames) from immediate area. [erg2024-en-pdf#p189]
  • All equipment used when handling the product must be grounded. [erg2024-en-pdf#p189]
  • Do not touch or walk through spilled material. [erg2024-en-pdf#p189]
  • Stop leak if you can do it without risk. [erg2024-en-pdf#p189]
  • Prevent entry into waterways, sewers, basements or confined areas. [erg2024-en-pdf#p189]
  • A vapor-suppressing foam may be used to reduce vapors. [erg2024-en-pdf#p189]
  • Absorb or cover with dry earth, sand or other non-combustible material and transfer to containers. [erg2024-en-pdf#p189]
  • Use clean, non-sparking tools to collect absorbed material. [erg2024-en-pdf#p189]

Large Spill

  • Dike far ahead of liquid spill for later disposal. [erg2024-en-pdf#p189]
  • Water spray may reduce vapor, but may not prevent ignition in closed spaces. [erg2024-en-pdf#p189]

FIRST AID

Refer to the “General First Aid” section.

Specific First Aid:

  • Wash skin with soap and water. [erg2024-en-pdf#p189]
  • In case of burns, immediately cool affected skin for as long as possible with cold water.Do not remove clothing if adhering to skin. [erg2024-en-pdf#p189]
