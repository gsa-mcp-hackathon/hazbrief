Several analyses suggest that it will take 5 to 10 minutes on average to implement shelter-in-place, once a sheltering decision is made by a household. Sorensen, Shumpert and Vogt (2002), ORNL/TM-2002/144, p. 22
a considerable amount of time (up to one to two hours using conventional warning practices) may elapse before most people in the affected area hear, absorb, and decide to respond to the instructions Sorensen, Shumpert and Vogt (2002), ORNL/TM-2002/144, p. 21
